Student Innovation Project

EdgeReview — an AI-assisted trading journal analyzer.

A web tool where a trader uploads their journal and receives, in seconds, a complete quantitative performance breakdown and a plain-language review of where their edge is — and where it leaks.

Innovation Claim

EdgeReview is the first tool to pair full automated trade-performance analytics with an AI reviewer that reads a trader's own results and explains them like a coach — bridging the gap between raw statistics calculators and generic trading advice, while keeping every byte of the user's data private on their own device.

Project Description

The problem, and how EdgeReview solves it.

Most traders track every trade and analyze none of them. Spreadsheets are intimidating, and the few analytics tools that exist hand back numbers without telling you what they mean. EdgeReview closes that loop in three steps.

Upload

The trader drops in a CSV export from any platform — cTrader, MT5, TradingView, a broker statement, or a hand-kept journal.

Quantify

EdgeReview computes the metrics that actually matter: profit factor, expectancy, payoff ratio, drawdown, streaks, and risk-adjusted return.

Review

An AI layer reads those results and returns a written assessment — the strengths, the leaks, and concrete things to test next.

The prototype — what I'm building.

The EdgeReview engine is in active development. This is the analysis it will deliver once a journal is uploaded — the metrics it computes, the report it generates, and how it keeps your data private.

EdgeReview Engine in development

100% local · no upload

Metrics it will compute

  • Net P&L & expectancy per trade
  • Win rate, profit factor, payoff ratio
  • Average & largest win / loss
  • Maximum drawdown & recovery factor
  • Win / loss streaks
  • Per-trade Sharpe (risk-adjusted return)

What it will return

  • A cumulative equity curve of your P&L
  • A metrics dashboard at a glance
  • A written AI review of your edge — strengths, leaks, and what to test next
  • A verdict: is the edge confirmed, marginal, or unproven?

Educational use only — not financial advice.
